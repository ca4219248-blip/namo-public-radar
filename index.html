Yeh system kya hai — aur kya NAHI

Yeh publicly available schedule data ka inference engine hai: announcements, timings aur live clock ko mila kar batata hai ki PM publicly kahan hone chahiye. Jab event live hota hai, exact venue dikhta hai.

Yeh real-time GPS tracking nahi hai — PM SPG protection mein hain aur unki live location koi legitimate source publish nahi karta. "Andaza" marker ek educated guess hai, exact position nahi.
